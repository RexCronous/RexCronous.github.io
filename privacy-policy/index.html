Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hermes Gmail Digest (“the App”) handles information when it is authorized by a Google account. The App is operated by RexCronous.

What the App does

Hermes Gmail Digest is a personal productivity assistant. When authorized, it reads relevant Gmail messages and may use Google Calendar, Google Sheets, Google Docs, and Google Drive data to produce summaries or support requested productivity workflows. The App does not sell data or use Google user data for advertising.

Google data we access

Depending on the features being used and the permissions granted, the App may access:

  • Gmail message metadata and message content needed to create an inbox digest;
  • Calendar event information needed for calendar-related workflows;
  • Spreadsheet, document, and Drive file data needed for explicitly requested workflows; and
  • Basic account information provided by Google to identify the authorized account.

The App requests only the scopes configured in its Google OAuth consent screen. It does not access data outside the permissions granted by the account owner.

How we use information

We use accessed information only to provide the App’s requested productivity features, such as preparing an email digest, reading or updating an authorized spreadsheet, or carrying out an explicitly requested calendar, document, or Drive operation. We do not use Google user data for targeted advertising, credit scoring, or unrelated profiling.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials and temporary application state may be stored on the operator’s secured server or workstation so the App can continue operating without repeated consent. Generated summaries and requested outputs may be retained in the destination chosen by the account owner. We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested feature, maintain security, or comply with legal obligations.

Sharing and service providers

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information may be processed by infrastructure and software services used to operate the App, only as necessary to provide the requested feature. We do not share Google user data with advertisers or data brokers. We may disclose information when required by law or when necessary to protect the App, its users, or others from abuse or security threats.

Security

We use access controls, least-privilege permissions, HTTPS for web traffic, and protected storage for OAuth credentials where supported. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Your choices and deletion requests

You can revoke the App’s Google access at any time from your Google Account security settings. You may request deletion of application-stored personal data by contacting privacy@cronous.my.id. We will delete data we control unless retention is required for security, legal, or dispute-resolution purposes. Revoking access may prevent the App from providing features that require the revoked permissions.

Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App, its data practices, or applicable requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when a new version is published.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests can be sent to privacy@cronous.my.id.
